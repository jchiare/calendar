Why families choose us

Evidence-based therapy, delivered with warmth

Expert care that feels like play, so kids stay excited to come back each week.

A plan for your child

No two kids communicate the same way. We start from your child’s strengths and interests and build a plan around the goals that matter to your family.

Expert therapists

Every clinician is a licensed, certified speech-language pathologist who keeps training in the latest evidence-based approaches.

Play-based learning

Kids learn best when they’re having fun. Games, stories, and pretend play keep them motivated — and give you easy ways to practice at home.

What we help with

Support for every kind of communicator

From first words to clear sounds to confident conversation, our team works across the full range of pediatric speech and language needs.

See all services
  • ArticulationSaying individual speech sounds clearly.
  • Apraxia of SpeechPlanning and coordinating the movements for speech.
  • Expressive LanguageUsing words and sentences to share ideas and needs.
  • Receptive LanguageUnderstanding words, directions, and questions.
  • Phonological DisordersSound patterns, like dropping or swapping sounds.
  • Social / PragmaticsConversation, turn-taking, and social cues.
  • Autism Spectrum DisordersSupporting communication, connection, and play.
  • Auditory ProcessingMaking sense of what is heard, especially in busy places.
  • Oral Motor DysfunctionStrength and coordination of lips, tongue, and jaw.
  • Parent CoachingStrategies that turn everyday routines into practice.

Getting started

Three simple steps

Not sure whether your child needs speech therapy? That’s exactly what the first call is for.

  1. Free consultation

    Book a free phone call. Tell us what you’re noticing and we’ll talk through next steps together.

  2. Assessment

    A licensed SLP evaluates your child’s communication skills and builds a personalized plan with clear goals.

  3. Weekly therapy

    Consistent weekly sessions, simple home practice, and goal reviews with you every six months.
