About us

Helping kids find their voice

Little Lantern opened in 2012 as a two-room practice. Today our team supports hundreds of local families, pairing current research with care that keeps each child at the center.

Our story

Built around families

Our founder and clinical director, Maya Okafor, spent a decade working in schools and hospitals before opening Little Lantern. She wanted a place where therapy felt like play and parents were part of every step.

That idea still shapes everything we do: therapy that is individualized, family-centered, and full of play — where children build confidence through connection, and parents leave every session knowing how to help at home.

What every family can expect

  • A personalized plan built around your child’s personality, strengths, and goals.
  • Parents as partners, with practical techniques and simple homework for carryover at home.
  • Clear goals set at the start of services and reviewed with you every six months.
  • Team coordination with your child’s teachers and other providers.
  • Flexible scheduling: morning, afternoon, and school or day care appointments.
