Therapy areas

What we treat

  • ArticulationSaying individual speech sounds clearly.
  • Apraxia of SpeechPlanning and coordinating the movements for speech.
  • Expressive LanguageUsing words and sentences to share ideas and needs.
  • Receptive LanguageUnderstanding words, directions, and questions.
  • Phonological DisordersSound patterns, like dropping or swapping sounds.
  • Social / PragmaticsConversation, turn-taking, and social cues.
  • Autism Spectrum DisordersSupporting communication, connection, and play.
  • Auditory ProcessingMaking sense of what is heard, especially in busy places.
  • Oral Motor DysfunctionStrength and coordination of lips, tongue, and jaw.
  • Parent CoachingStrategies that turn everyday routines into practice.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I know if my child needs speech therapy?

If you have questions about how your child talks, understands, or interacts, book a free consultation. We’ll listen to what you’re noticing and recommend whether a screening or assessment makes sense.

How long and how often are sessions?

Individual therapy sessions are 30 minutes, once a week. A steady routine helps skills stick.

When are appointments available?

Morning, afternoon, and school or day care appointments are available. Our office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

What happens if we can’t make a session?

For sessions your family can’t attend, we offer phone check-in home programs so your child’s progress keeps moving.

How will I know my child is making progress?

Goals are set at the start of services and reviewed with you every six months. You’ll also get simple home practice after sessions, so you can see and support progress along the way.

Do you work with insurance?

When you contact us, share your insurance provider (or let us know you’ll self-pay) and we’ll walk you through your options.
